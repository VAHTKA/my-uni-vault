My Uni Vault is a free student project that lets students store their study files and plan their week. This page explains what information it uses and why.

What we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID. We do not see your Google password, and we do not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data.
  • What you add: files and folders you upload, timetable entries you write, and your settings (display name, time zone, theme).

How it is used

  • To sign you in and keep your vault private to your account.
  • To show your files, timetable and settings back to you.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone except the services needed to run the site (below).

The site owner can see each account's name, email, storage use and file list, and can open or remove files when needed to keep the site safe (for example, to remove illegal or abusive content) or to manage storage.

Where it is stored

Files, timetable entries and settings are stored in Supabase Storage. Sign-in is handled by Google. The website runs on a hosting provider. Each user's data is kept in a separate private area and other users cannot see it. Preview and download links for your files expire after one hour.

Cookies

We use a single sign-in cookie to keep you logged in for up to 30 days. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes all of your files, timetable entries and settings. You can also remove My Uni Vault's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
